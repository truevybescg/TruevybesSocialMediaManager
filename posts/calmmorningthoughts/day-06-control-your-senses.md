# Day 6 — Control Your Senses Before They Control You

**Theme:** Discipline over distraction
**Gita Reference:** Chapter 2, Verses 60–61

## Shloka

> इन्द्रियाणि प्रमाथीनि हरन्ति प्रसभं मनः।
> तानि सर्वाणि संयम्य युक्त आसीत मत्परः॥

**Transliteration:** Indriyani pramathini, haranti prasabham manah,
Tani sarvani samyamya, yukta asita matparah.

**Simple meaning:** Indriyan (senses) bahut prabal hoti hain, ye zabardasti
mann ko bhi kheench leti hain. Isliye inpe control rakhna zaroori hai, aur
apna focus sthir rakhna zaroori hai.

choose karo, // reactively nahi, consciously.
