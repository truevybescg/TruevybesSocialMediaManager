# Day 1 — Do The Work, Let Go Of The Outcome

**Theme:** Nishkama Karma (कर्म करो, फल की चिंता मत करो)
**Gita Reference:** Chapter 2, Verse 47

## Shloka

> कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन।
> मा कर्मफलहेतुर्भूर्मा ते सङ्गोऽस्त्वकर्मणि॥

**Transliteration:** Karmanye vadhikaraste, Ma phaleshu kadachana,
Ma karmaphalaheturbhurma te sangostvakarmani.

**Simple meaning:** Tumhara adhikaar sirf apne karm par hai, uske phal (result)
par nahi. Toh sirf result ke liye kaam mat karo, aur na hi kaam karna hi
chhod do.

## Script (voiceover, ~60–75 sec)

Kabhi socha hai // hum itna stress kyun lete hain? Interview ho, exam ho,
ya koi bada decision — hum result ke baare mein itna sochte hain ki
present moment hi chhoot jaata hai.

Shri Krishna ne Arjun se yehi kaha tha, battlefield ke beech mein //
"Karmanye vadhikaraste, ma phaleshu kadachana."

Iska matlab hai — tumhara haq sirf apne karm par hai, uske result par
nahi. // Tum apna 100% de sakte ho, lekin outcome — woh universe decide
karega.

Toh agla presentation ho, ya koi mushkil conversation // apna best do,
aur phal ki chinta chhod do. // Yehi Gita ka pehla lesson hai peace ka —
control jo tumhare haath mein hai, uspe focus karo. // Baaki sab, chhod
do.

Aaj se try karo — kaam karo pure dil se, // result ko universe ke haath
mein chhod do.
