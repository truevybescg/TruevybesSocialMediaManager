# Day 3 — The Soul Never Dies

**Theme:** Releasing the fear of loss
**Gita Reference:** Chapter 2, Verse 20

## Shloka

> न जायते म्रियते वा कदाचिन्नायं भूत्वा भविता वा न भूयः।
> अजो नित्यः शाश्वतोऽयं पुराणो न हन्यते हन्यमाने शरीरे॥

**Transliteration:** Na jayate mriyate va kadachin, nayam bhutva bhavita
va na bhuyah, Ajo nityah shashvato 'yam purano, na hanyate hanyamane
sharire.

**Simple meaning:** Aatma na kabhi janmti hai, na kabhi marti hai. Ye
sanatan hai, hamesha se hai aur hamesha rahegi. Sharir khatam ho sakta
hai, par aatma nahi.

## Script (voiceover, ~60–75 sec)

Kisi apne ko khone ka dar, // ya khud ki mrityu ka dar — ye sabse gehra
dar hai jo insaan ke andar hota hai.

Krishna Arjun se kehte hain // "Na jayate mriyate va kadachin" — tumhari
aatma kabhi paida nahi hoti, aur kabhi marti nahi. // Ye hamesha thi,
hamesha rahegi.

Jo cheez khatam hoti hai, woh sirf sharir hai — // jaise ek kapda jo hum
pehen ke utaar dete hain. // Par jo andar hai, woh anant hai, avinashi
hai.

Iska matlab ye nahi ki hum dukh na maanein — // dukh manana insaniyat
hai. // Lekin iss gyaan ke saath, dar thoda kam ho jaata hai. // Kyunki
jisse hum pyaar karte hain, uski aatma kahin nahi jaati.

Aaj agar koi khaali jagah mehsoos ho, // ye yaad rakhna — // pyaar aur
connection, woh sharir se pare hote hain.
